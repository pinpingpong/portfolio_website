A persistent Claude Project holding my resume and four years of peer evaluations, kept private and used only by me. Every answer I prepared was mapped back to real evidence in it: a specific project, a specific piece of peer feedback. Nothing invented. That same corpus judged fit — comparing each JD against my actual documented track record to decide whether a role was genuinely the right next step, not just one I could land.
