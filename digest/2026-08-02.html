Microsoft Goes Its
Own Way, Acquisitions
Pile Up

Microsoft pitches homegrown MAI models on its own Maia chips as a cheaper alternative to OpenAI and Anthropic, a wave of acquisitions reshapes coding and robotics, and agent frameworks push toward fully local execution.

Big News
Microsoft stops just hosting AI and starts competing with it

Microsoft is now selling its own homegrown MAI model family, running on its own Maia chips, pitched directly to Wall Street as a cheaper alternative to OpenAI and Anthropic — a notable shift from its role as the biggest reseller of both.

MicrosoftInfrastructure
Three acquisitions in one week reshuffle coding and robotics

AI coding startup Cognition bought Poke, the AI assistant that texts like a friend; World Labs acquired robotics company SceniX to test its simulations on real hardware; and Midjourney bought astrology app Co-Star as part of a push into consumer apps beyond image generation.

M&ARobotics
DeepSeek ships V4-Flash, frontier releases keep coming every 2 days

DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 landed July 31, the latest in a release cadence that's now averaging a new frontier or near-frontier model every two days across labs — a pace that shows no sign of slowing heading into August.

DeepSeekRelease Cadence
Kalanick's Atoms raises $1.7B for physical AI

Atoms, the physical AI startup founded by Uber's Travis Kalanick, closed a $1.7B round led by Andreessen Horowitz — the largest raise of the week, ahead of $400M for 3D-model startup Meshy AI and $300M for chip co-designer Etched.
