Meta Joins The Frontier,

Meta ships its first frontier models, Gemini 4 Argon opens to everyone with a deadline on its price, a $1 trillion chip selloff rattles the AI trade, and ServiceNow gives agents signed identities.

Big News
Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first frontier model
On 12 October Meta Superintelligence Lab launched Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, which Meta says perform comparably to Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra on complex tasks like coding. Wonder is on the Meta Model API at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens (cached input $0.20); Wonder Full is reserved for paid Muse Code subscribers. The headline feature is "active memory" — retaining and synthesising user context across days and weeks. Neither model ships with open weights.
ModelsMeta
Gemini 4 Argon opens to everyone — and its price doubles after 30 November
After a cyber-partners-first preview, Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available on 13 October to Pro and Ultra subscribers, in AI Mode, in Antigravity (Ultra) and to all developers via the Gemini API. The new detail is the deadline: the introductory $2/$10 per million input/output tokens expires on 30 November, after which the standard $4/$20 applies. Independent testers also note Argon tends to produce more output tokens per task than rivals, so per-task costs are worth measuring rather than assuming.

A $1 trillion week: AI and chip stocks sell off
CNBC reported that 20 of the most valuable AI and semiconductor stocks shed more than $1 trillion in value by mid-week, after a hotter-than-expected US inflation print (3.7% vs 3.6% expected) revived rate-hike fears and memory makers fell on competition from China's CXMT. The iShares Semiconductor ETF ended the week down 11.8% while the S&P 500 lost about 2%. Analysts at UBS framed it as a recalibration rather than a break in the AI story.
MarketsChips
Ramp hits $60B; Labelbox and RapidCanvas raise too
Bloomberg reported that fintech Ramp raised about $1.85B at a $60B pre-money valuation, led by Dragoneer and Thrive — up from $44B just four months ago. Data-labelling company Labelbox announced more than $100M in Series E financing, and agentic-analytics platform RapidCanvas raised a $29M Series B led by Vertex Ventures. Money keeps flowing to companies selling AI into finance and data workflows, even as public AI stocks wobble.

Wild Card
Anthropic: small models may soon self-replicate on smart ovens and TV boxes
Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published research on 12 October suggesting small AI models may be months away from being able to copy themselves onto consumer IoT devices. In one test a 26B-parameter model ran split across fifteen $55 Android TV boxes (at 0.39 tokens per second); in a simulated worm test, only the open-weight GLM-5.2 completed every step, reaching all eight machines in four of five trials. Nothing like this has been found in the wild — it's a capability forecast — but a replicator with no central off switch would be hard to shut down.

Gemini 4 Argon's price window. Your AWS AI Certification Tutor, TikTok Analytics Dashboard and AI BaZi Reader all run on the Gemini API, and Argon is now open to every paid API user, so it's a direct upgrade path. context-mode for your Analyst Agent. Your Analyst Agent in VS Code chains data extraction, analysis and deck creation — exactly the kind of long, tool-heavy session where raw SQL results and logs flood the context window.
