Grab

Five frontier models land in two weeks, Nvidia moves to own the open-weight commons, and OpenAI stakes its claim on agents as the default way to control anything.

Big News
Nvidia moves to buy Hugging Face for $13B, tightening its grip on open-weight AI
Nvidia is reportedly in talks to acquire Hugging Face, the platform hosting most of the world's open-weight models and benchmarks, for roughly $13 billion. It's the clearest sign yet that owning the distribution layer for open models is becoming as strategically valuable as owning the chips underneath them.
M&AInfrastructure
Five frontier labs ship new flagships inside two weeks
Anthropic opened September with Claude Fable 5.1 and an invite-only Claude Mythos 5.1, Google and Meta followed a day later with Gemini 3.8 Flash and Muse Spark 1.3, OpenAI answered with GPT-6 Astra on September 3, and Sakana AI closed the run with Fugu Ultra v2.0 on September 11. Eleven new models have shipped from seven providers so far this month.
ModelsAnthropicOpenAI
OpenAI opens its Agents API, betting agents become the default control surface
OpenAI exposed its Agents API and ChatGPT Work agent infrastructure this week, a move aimed squarely at device and hardware makers. The pitch: instead of building bespoke integrations for every product, let an agent be the interface — for your home, your desk, your workflow.
AgentsOpenAI
AI funding keeps setting records: $11B across 18 rounds in a single week
Poolside raised $6B led by Nvidia, Mistral closed $3.5B at a $24.4B valuation, and Cognition — maker of the Devin coding agent — raised $2B at a $48B valuation, all in the week of September 7–13. Legal AI startup Harvey hit a $15.5B valuation, months after its last raise.

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash
DeepSeek · new release
The single tracked model release of the past seven days — an efficiency-focused flash variant continuing DeepSeek's push on cost-per-token rather than raw benchmark scores.
Wild Card
Bending Spoons bought Miro for $1.3B — a fraction of its 2022 peak valuation
Serial acquirer Bending Spoons picked up collaboration-board maker Miro for $1.3 billion, a steep markdown from the $17.5 billion Miro was valued at in 2022. A quiet reminder that in the AI era, even well-loved SaaS tools without a clear agentic story are getting repriced.
