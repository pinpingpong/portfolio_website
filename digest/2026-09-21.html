Rivals Huddle On
Safety, A Model
Skips Language

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google quietly align on how fast frontier AI should move, a non-LLM model from RLHF's inventor stuns developers with 5–18x speedups, and long-running agent infrastructure pulls in half a billion dollars.

Big News
OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google quietly align on how fast AI should move
The three labs have been holding closed-door safety talks for weeks, even as the Trump administration pushes to keep pace with China. Industry leaders are warming to parts of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's call to deliberately "pace" frontier development — a shift from vague concern statements toward a proposed industry standards body.

Salesforce ships its first reasoning model, built on Nvidia's open Nemotron
Koa, unveiled at Dreamforce, is Salesforce's first reasoning model and runs on Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron base — a sign that enterprise software vendors increasingly build on open foundations rather than paying frontier-lab API prices for every workflow.
ModelsEnterprise
Google DeepMind opens an institute dedicated to the AGI debate
The new institute aims to widen the conversation about artificial general intelligence beyond the labs racing to build it, pulling outside researchers and policy voices into a discussion that's mostly happened behind closed doors.
AGIResearch
Unsealed filings: a Microsoft exec called AI scraping "the largest theft of labor in history"
Newly unredacted court documents show Microsoft privately worried about OpenAI's data practices even as both companies scraped paywalled New York Times content to build training datasets — and warned internally it would gut publishers.

Tools Worth Knowing
Temporal Technologies
$550M Series E · $12.55B valuation
Open source platform for building and running long-running AI agents with durable execution — the infrastructure layer that keeps agent workflows alive across crashes, retries, and multi-day tasks.
Microsoft Copilot Cowork
Now generally available
Brings long-running, multi-step planning and workflow execution into Microsoft 365. Researcher also gains Critique and Council modes for comparing outputs across models before committing to one.
Ponytail
~20K GitHub stars
A coding-agent add-on built around one idea — make the agent do less unnecessary work. Instead of maximizing thoroughness, it trains agents to act like an efficient senior developer who knows when to stop.
Arcee AI
$150M Series B · $1B+ valuation
Builds open-weight models for enterprise deployment, betting that companies increasingly want models they can run and fine-tune themselves rather than call through an API.
Wild Card
A model that skips language entirely and just outputs probabilities
TypeSafe AI, founded by the OpenAI researcher who invented RLHF, released Jev — a transformer that outputs calibrated probability scores instead of generating text token by token, sidestepping hallucination by design. Vercel saw 5–18x speedups replacing an OpenAI model for command-safety checks; Bryo AI found it 10–20x cheaper than Gemini for email classification. Demand was high enough that TypeSafe had to throttle API access.

For You — Pin Xiu
Personalised picks

Temporal Technologies' $550M raise is worth watching because it's exactly the infrastructure gap behind your own scheduled agents — the Weekly AI Digest Agent and the Analyst Agent both run long, multi-step jobs on a schedule. Temporal's durable-execution model (retry safely, survive crashes, resume mid-task) is the pattern to reach for once a Claude routine or SKILL.md SOP needs to outlive a single session.

TypeSafe AI's Jev is directly relevant to the guardrailed NL-to-SQL layer in your Content Analytics Agent and the Snowflake queries the Analyst Agent generates. A narrow, cheap, fast model that just scores "is this safe to run" instead of writing prose is the kind of component that could replace a full LLM call in a validation step — same idea as your SQL guardrails, but purpose-built and 10-20x cheaper.

Microsoft Copilot Cowork going GA matters for the day job — as long-running agentic work becomes a standard Microsoft 365 feature rather than something you build from scratch, it shifts what's worth custom-building versus buying off the shelf. Useful context as you scope the next wave of AI Agentic products for GXS.
