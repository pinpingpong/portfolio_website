Issue #17 · 01 Nov 2026 · Weekly AI Digest

Anthropic Files To Go Public,
OpenAI Trims Its Round,
Agents Break In

Anthropic's S-1 shows $16.3B in half-year revenue, OpenAI cuts its raise to $20B as AI stocks slide, Apple picks Qwen for China, and a startup's agents found a way into Anthropic's business systems in under 12 hours.

Big News
Anthropic files for a Nasdaq IPO under the ticker "ANT"
Anthropic's S-1, filed on 26 October, reports $16.29B in revenue for the six months to 30 June, up 1,304% from $1.16B a year earlier, alongside an $80.7B net loss that includes a $72.4B non-cash charge. About 80% of revenue ($13.1B) is consumption-based API usage and 20% ($3.2B) comes from subscriptions; international revenue rose to $4.5B. In Q2 alone it spent $5.6B on compute against $11.6B of revenue. The filing doesn't yet set a share price or offering size.

OpenAI pares its mega-round to $20B as the AI trade cools
The Financial Times reported, and CNBC confirmed on 31 October, that OpenAI has cut its current raise from about $30B to about $20B and will seek a $1.25 trillion post-money valuation, down from the $1.4 trillion floated in September. The WSJ says around $11B is committed so far, with Coatue and UAE investors leading. Coverage ties the cut to a tech selloff that has knocked the Nasdaq 100 roughly 10% off its 6 October record; Fortune noted Meta received a credit warning over its AI spending the same week.

Apple picks Alibaba's Qwen to power Apple Intelligence in China
Apple confirmed on 31 October that Qwen will run inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for users in China. Testing starts in the iOS developer beta on 4 November, with a public rollout in iOS 27.3 in December. Users opt in and choose what to share, and Alibaba says it won't train on user requests or responses. The next-generation Siri features shown at WWDC26 aren't part of this first China release.

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a world model with native audio
Announced on 27 October as a research preview for trusted testers, Genie 4 generates interactive worlds in real time, with ambient sound and characters that talk, and lets you steer a running scene by typing — add characters, move the camera, change the weather. Gemini models keep the simulations coherent. Google says details can drift over long sessions. It plans to bring Genie 4 to AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and to early-access Gemini API partners "in the coming weeks"; no pricing or benchmarks are out yet.

Tools Worth Knowing
lightpanda-io/browser
Zig · ~38.8k ★
A headless browser for AI agents and automation, written from scratch in Zig rather than forked from Chromium, with V8 for JavaScript. The team's own benchmarks claim 11× faster execution and 9× less memory than headless Chrome. It was on GitHub's trending list this week.
CopilotKit/OpenBot
MIT · 3.5k+ ★ · trending 30 Oct
Self-hosted "AI coworkers" that each get their own sandboxed computer — a browser, files and tools — where every action is approved before it runs and logged afterwards. Any AG-UI agent can plug in, and it runs on Docker with Postgres and pgvector. Still early (v0.0.5).
Gemini app: edit files in chat
Google · 29 Oct · all users
Gemini can now preview and edit the files it generates without leaving the chat — Docs, Sheets, slides, PDFs, Markdown, LaTeX and code. Highlight a section to rewrite it, or edit it by hand in full screen. On phones, only PDFs can be edited for now.
ServiceNow plugin for ChatGPT
OpenAI × ServiceNow · 28 Oct
The expanded partnership adds a ChatGPT plugin that runs ServiceNow workflows from the chat while keeping ServiceNow's permissions in place. OpenAI agents can also call workflows through Action Fabric. A joint security product is due in early access in January 2027.
Wild Card
OpenAI claims its internal models cracked problems in maths, CS and physics
On 26 October OpenAI said unreleased internal models produced three results: a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension, a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and a rigorous existence proof for four-dimensional quantum field theories including QCD and pure Yang–Mills, which is part of a Clay Millennium Prize problem. There are big caveats: the separate Yang–Mills mass-gap question still appears to be open, the physics materials haven't been released, and outside verification is being led by researchers at UC San Diego and MIT. If the results hold up, it would be a remarkable week for AI in mathematics.

Personalised picks

Cathedral's agents got into Anthropic in under 12 hours. In a bug-bounty test, human researchers spent days failing to get in. AI agents then found a way within 12 hours — through flaws in third-party business software, not the model. Anthropic says the access was limited to some sales, HR and IT systems and disputes Cathedral's framing. For the agentic products you're shipping at GXS with Grab, the lesson is that attackers now run agents too. Your threat model needs to cover the SaaS tools your agents connect to, not just prompt injection.

Sweden blocks ChatGPT and Claude, keeps Copilot. From 26 October, Sweden's Government Offices blocked the browser versions of both chatbots on work PCs because they lacked enterprise contracts. Staff were sent to a vetted Copilot and to an internally procured platform that still serves the same models. A regulated bank will usually draw the line in the same place: approved channels, not banned models. That is a strong argument for how your Analyst Agent is built — it runs on Copilot in VS Code with Snowflake access through governed credentials, the kind of setup that survives a crackdown on unsanctioned AI tools.

OpenBot's "approve before, log after" pattern. Your MapleStory Daily Agent already writes an audit-friendly report and has hard guardrails like zero gem spending. OpenBot builds the same idea into infrastructure: each agent gets its own sandboxed computer, and every action is checked before it runs and recorded afterwards. Pair it with a lightweight browser like Lightpanda and you have a good template for agents that touch banking data. It also gives you a clear story for interviews about safe autonomy.

Anthropic's IPO becomes the market's AI test
Anthropic's S-1 lands in the same week OpenAI shrank its round and the Nasdaq 100 slid about 10% from its peak, so pricing will show what public investors will pay for frontier AI once the losses are visible. Watch for the price range and the roadshow — trading is reported to be targeted for mid-to-late November, though not confirmed. Also note the revenue mix: 80% of Anthropic's revenue comes from metered API usage, which means it is driven by agents and developers rather than chat subscriptions.
