Big News
Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 and raises a record $81B
Anthropic priced its IPO on 18 November at $83 a share, above the $70–78 range from its roadshow, and upsized the offering from 825 million to 980 million shares. That raises about $81.3B, more than SpaceX's $75B listing and the largest IPO on record, according to CNBC. The stock started trading on Nasdaq as "ANT" on Thursday 19 November, opened at $111.50 (34% above the IPO price) and closed its first day at $97.17. It fell around 9% on day two but stayed above $83. Market cap estimates vary depending on how shares are counted: CNBC put the fully diluted figure around $1.5 trillion, while Yahoo Finance's close-based number was about $1.27 trillion. The debut came during a broader market slide rather than stopping it.

Google ships Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per million tokens
On 18 November Google released Gemini 4 Neon, which The Decoder calls its fourth new Gemini model in a month. It's paid-only with no free tier, available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and the Pro and Ultra subscriptions, with a context window of about one million tokens. Pricing reported across several outlets is $3 input and $15 output per million tokens (thinking tokens included), flat across the whole window, with implicitly cached input at $0.15. Neon sits between Hydrogen ($1.50/$7.50) and Carbon. That matters because Carbon and Argon's introductory $2/$10 rate doubles to $4/$20 on 1 December, which leaves Neon about 25% cheaper than Carbon from next month. Most of these figures come from third-party coverage rather than Google's own pricing page.

Microsoft Ignite: Copilot becomes a platform for agents with their own IDs
At Ignite (Book of News dated 17 November), Microsoft made Copilot Home, which merges Chat and Cowork, and Office in Copilot (Word, Excel and PowerPoint inside Copilot) generally available. Copilot Autopilot, in the Frontier program, gives each autopilot its own Microsoft Entra Agent ID and an assigned human manager. Agent Accounts, adding memory and triggers so agents can run persistently, enter public preview in December. Agent 365 adds governance for MCP servers and "shadow" agents in preview, and Microsoft Defender now scans local AI coding tools such as Claude Code and GitHub Copilot CLI for hidden malicious instructions. Coverage disagrees on a few general availability dates, so check the Book of News before planning around them.

Nvidia stops shipping Hopper chips to China
On its earnings call on 18 November, Nvidia's interim CEO Colette Kress said the company had sold $2.4B of licensed Hopper-generation data centre products, including the H200, to China in its fiscal third quarter, but that its guidance "does not have any licensed Hopper data centre products included". The roughly $130B fourth-quarter forecast excludes AI chip and system sales to China. Nvidia didn't say why it stopped or whether the halt is permanent, and Beijing has also been restricting H200 imports. Separately, Deutsche Telekom's T.Capital fund put €100M (about $110M) into Mistral's roughly €3B Series D on 19 November, the start of a partnership to sell Mistral's models to German companies and public bodies.

Tools Worth Knowing
dream-num/univer
TypeScript · ~31.1k ★ (+7.2k this week)
An office SDK for building AI-enabled spreadsheets, documents and slides into your own apps, with editing, formula calculation and real-time collaboration. It gained more stars than any other repo on GitHub's weekly trending list, as Anthropic, Microsoft and others race to put AI inside office files.
alibaba/open-code-review
Go · ~55.1k ★ (+4.0k this week)
Alibaba's open-source code reviewer combines deterministic checks with an LLM agent and leaves line-level comments on your changes. Pairing fixed rules with a model is a sensible way to catch what an agent misses without trusting it to mark its own work.
backnotprop/plannotator
TypeScript · ~14.2k ★ (+3.4k this week)
A workspace for planning, annotating and reviewing code with coding agents. You mark up an agent's plan or diff, and your comments are sent back to the agent, so feedback becomes part of the loop instead of a chat message it might ignore.
ag-ui-protocol/ag-ui
TypeScript · ~21.0k ★ (+3.8k this week)
The Agent-User Interaction Protocol, an open standard for connecting agents to front-end apps: streaming messages, tool calls and shared state between an agent backend and the UI. It's the layer that sits alongside MCP (agent to tools) and A2A (agent to agent).
Wild Card
An AI pentesting tool turned up in a hack on the European Commission
The European Commission confirmed this week that a cyberattack took its HR systems offline for more than 10 days, hitting payroll, leave requests, medical reimbursements and translation services. CERT-EU found ARTEX on its systems, an open-source tool that uses LLMs to coordinate several agents probing for weaknesses. ARTEX was built for authorised security testing, and it's still unclear whether it helped the attackers get in or only mapped weak spots. No attacker has been named, and a spokesperson said there was "no evidence of a data breach affecting Commission staff as a whole" while investigators check whether any employee data was taken. It's the Commission's third disclosed cyber incident this year, and one of the clearest public cases of a multi-agent testing tool being used against a real government target.

For You — Pin Xiu
Personalised picks

Check your Gemini bills before 1 December. Most of your live apps, the Content Analytics Agent, the AI Twin, the AWS Tutor, the TikTok dashboard, the Renovation Agent and the BaZi Reader, run on the Gemini API. If any of them use Carbon or Argon, their token price doubles to $4/$20 next week. Neon at $3/$15 or Hydrogen at $1.50/$7.50 may be good enough for NL-to-SQL and RAG answers, and since they're all on Streamlit's free tier with light traffic, a quick side-by-side on a few real questions should tell you which to pin.

Agent identity is becoming standard practice. Microsoft giving each Copilot autopilot an Entra Agent ID and a named human manager, and adding governance for MCP servers and shadow agents, is the enterprise version of what you designed into your Analyst Agent: scoped Snowflake credentials, one install script, and outputs a person signs off on. Whatever your next role looks like, being able to explain how your agents are identified, owned and audited will count for as much in interviews as what they can do. Defender now scanning Claude Code for hidden instructions is also a reminder to keep your SKILL.md files and scheduled routines tidy.

Ideas worth borrowing for your own agents. mvanhorn/last30days-skill (~72k ★) is an agent skill that researches a topic across Reddit, X, YouTube, Hacker News and the web, then summarises it: close to what this digest agent does, and worth reading for its source handling. univer is a ready-made spreadsheet and document layer if you ever want the Content Analytics Agent to hand back an editable sheet instead of a chart, and plannotator's annotate-and-send-back loop would suit reviewing your agents' plans before they touch real data.
