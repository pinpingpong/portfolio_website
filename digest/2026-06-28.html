Issue #2 · 28 Jun 2026 · Weekly AI Digest

Fable 5 is here,
Qualcomm bets $4B,
agents ship to prod

Anthropic drops its biggest model yet, Qualcomm makes its boldest AI bet, and Microsoft's agent framework goes generally available — a week where the AI infrastructure layer finally solidified.

Big News
Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5
Anthropic released two new frontier models — Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 — representing a major capability tier above Claude Opus 4.8. Early evals show strong gains on coding, reasoning, and agentic tasks. The launch coincides with a separate Agent SDK billing update (June 15) that splits usage into a dedicated monthly credit pool on Pro and Max plans.
AnthropicModelsAgent SDK
Qualcomm to acquire AI startup Modular for ~$4B
Qualcomm is set to buy Modular, the startup behind the Mojo programming language and an alternative to PyTorch/CUDA, at nearly three times its September 2025 valuation of $1.6B. The deal signals that hardware companies are no longer content to sit below the AI software stack — Qualcomm is betting that owning the compiler layer is as important as owning the chip.
AcquisitionAI InfraQualcomm
Nobel laureate John Jumper leaves DeepMind for Anthropic
John Jumper, co-creator of AlphaFold and 2024 Nobel Prize winner in chemistry, has announced he's joining Anthropic after nearly nine years at Google DeepMind. The move underscores Anthropic's accelerating talent pull from the broader AI research ecosystem and adds serious scientific credibility to its alignment-focused mission.
TalentAnthropicResearch
Google DeepMind bets $75M on AI's future in Hollywood via A24
Google DeepMind is partnering with indie film studio A24 in a $75M deal to build AI-powered filmmaking tools. DeepMind's generative video and audio models will be integrated into A24's production pipeline. It's a major cultural signal: the most credible AI safety lab and the coolest film studio in the world are now building together.

Tools Worth Knowing
Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0
GA · Python + .NET · github.com/microsoft/agent-framework
AutoGen and Semantic Kernel have been merged into a single unified SDK. Supports multi-agent orchestration, evals, observability, and open-source governance. Went GA at Microsoft Build 2026 (June 2–3) with first-class Azure AI Foundry integration.
Gemini 3.5 Pro
Google DeepMind · 2M-token context · "Deep Think" mode
Gemini 3.5 Pro ships with a 2-million-token context window and a specialised "Deep Think" cognitive mode for extended reasoning tasks. The companion Gemini 3.5 Flash prioritises speed. Together they represent Google's biggest context-window bet yet.
OpenClaw
★ 210k+ GitHub stars · Local-first · 50+ integrations
The breakout open-source project of 2026 — a local AI assistant that runs entirely on your devices and acts as a gateway to 50+ integrations including WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Signal, and iMessage. Zero cloud dependency, full personal context.
MCP 2026-07-28 Release Candidate
200+ server implementations · Largest revision since launch
The Model Context Protocol crossed 200 server implementations and is now in a ten-week validation window for its biggest protocol revision since launch. Key additions include improved streaming, auth patterns, and multi-agent coordination primitives.
