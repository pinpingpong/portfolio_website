Model releases collide at the frontier, a Nobel laureate defects to Anthropic, and the agent framework era officially matures — all in the same week.

Big News
Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves DeepMind for Anthropic
John Jumper — who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his work on AlphaFold at DeepMind — is leaving Google after nearly 9 years to join Anthropic. The move is one of the highest-profile talent defections in AI history and underscores how aggressively Anthropic is recruiting elite scientific minds. Character AI co-founder Noam Shazeer also announced a departure from DeepMind this week, heading to OpenAI.

The Model Race Goes Vertical — Gemini Deep Think vs GPT-5.6
Google launched Gemini 2.5 Pro with "Deep Think" reasoning mode, calling it the most capable model it has ever released. OpenAI countered with a limited preview of the GPT-5.6 series: Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced, 2× cheaper than GPT-5.5), and Luna (fast and affordable). Two frontier model families dropped in the same week — and Terra's pricing shift may matter most for practitioners building pipelines.

Qualcomm to Acquire AI Startup Modular for ~$4B
Qualcomm is acquiring Modular, an AI infrastructure startup best known for the Mojo programming language and MAX inference engine, for roughly $4 billion — nearly triple its September 2025 valuation of $1.6B. The deal signals that chip companies are racing to own the full AI stack, not just the silicon.

★ 210,000+ GitHub stars
The fastest-growing open-source project in GitHub history. A local AI gateway connecting models to 50+ integrations — WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, iMessage, Discord — all running on your own hardware with no cloud required.
Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0
Generally Available — June 2026
Merges AutoGen and Semantic Kernel into a unified .NET and Python SDK. GA'd at Build 2026, it's Microsoft's official bet on multi-agent orchestration with built-in observability, evals, and open-source governance.
DeepSeek V4 (1.6T MoE)
Open-weight preview — June 2026
A 1.6-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts reasoning model in preview. Fully open-weight, aimed at matching frontier closed models on coding and heavy reasoning tasks. Continues DeepSeek's run as open-source's best response to GPT.
Zoom AI Productivity Suite
Launched 1 Jun 2026
Wraps Zoom Canvas (docs), Slides, Sheets, and Paper into one AI-powered workspace designed to turn meetings into deliverables. The pitch: full workflow completion, not just note-taking.
Wild Card
Google DeepMind Bets $75M on AI's Future in Hollywood via A24
Google DeepMind is partnering with indie prestige studio A24 and investing $75 million to build AI filmmaking tools. A24 is behind Everything Everywhere All at Once, Midsommar, and Hereditary — arguably the most creatively ambitious studio in Hollywood right now. This is the most serious real-money bet yet on generative AI as a tool for serious filmmakers rather than a content factory.

Personalised picks

Taktile — $110M Series C for Financial AI Decision Automation. You're building LightGBM models, churn prediction, and funnel analytics at GXS Bank. Taktile just raised $110M specifically to productionise automated decision flows in financial services — credit scoring, risk controls, real-time model serving. It's essentially a startup trying to productise what you're building internally. Worth watching how they're structuring model governance and auditability layers; those patterns tend to flow back into internal tooling at banks.

Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0 GA. You're running three active agents: the Analyst Agent (VS Code + Copilot + Snowflake/dbt), MapleStory automation (Claude computer use + Routines), and this digest. The GA merges AutoGen and Semantic Kernel into a single Python SDK with first-class support for multi-agent orchestration, observability, and evals. Even if you stay on Claude's Agent SDK, the patterns and tooling coming out of this ecosystem are worth benchmarking against — especially the eval frameworks for agents making data decisions.

Claude Agent SDK Billing Change — June 15. Starting June 15, Agent SDK usage draws from a separate monthly credit pool on subscription plans ($20 on Pro, $100 on Max 5×, $200 on Max 20×) rather than your interactive chat limit. This directly affects the digest agent and your MapleStory Routines. If you're on Pro, it's worth reviewing how many agent-hours those pipelines consume per month to decide whether a Max plan makes more economic sense.

Pick of the Week
01
Terra (GPT-5.6) — Frontier Reasoning at Half the Price
Two model families dropped at the frontier this week, but the single most practically interesting detail is Terra: OpenAI's balanced GPT-5.6 model that matches GPT-5.5 performance at 2× lower cost. For anyone building agent pipelines — where model calls stack up fast — that pricing shift changes the economic calculus entirely. Pair it with Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Think as the reasoning baseline and you've got a genuinely different cost-capability tradeoff curve than existed two weeks ago.
